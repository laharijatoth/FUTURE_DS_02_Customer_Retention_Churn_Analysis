## 📌 Project Overview
This repository contains the completed work for **Future Interns Data Science & Analytics – Task 2: Customer Retention & Churn Analysis**. 

In subscription-based businesses and SaaS models, understanding why customers leave and what keeps them engaged is critical to revenue growth. This project analyzes customer data to uncover churn patterns, evaluate cohort retention trends, identify key risk drivers, and propose actionable business recommendations.

---

## 📂 Repository Contents
* **`Customer_Retention_and_Churn_Analysis.xlsx`**: Professional multi-tab Excel workbook containing:
  * **Raw Data**: 1,500 customer records with demographics, subscription plans, contract types, tenure, monthly charges, and churn status.
  * **Executive Summary**: High-level KPIs including overall churn rate, average monthly revenue per user (ARPU), and total historical revenue.
  * **Cohort Retention**: Pivot table tracking customer signup cohorts across tenure months.
* **`Customer_Retention_and_Churn_Analysis_Report.md`**: Comprehensive analytical report formatted for stakeholders, product managers, and founders.

---

## 📊 Key Findings & Insights
1. **Overall Churn Rate**: The analysis revealed an overall customer churn rate of **~26.4%**, which represents a significant leak in recurring revenue.
2. **Contract Type Impact**: **Month-to-month subscribers** exhibit a churn rate nearly **3x higher** than annual contract holders, making contract length the single strongest leading indicator of churn.
3. **Tenure Vulnerability**: The highest drop-off occurs within the **first 3 to 6 months** of subscription (the onboarding cliff), indicating that early user habit formation is crucial.
4. **Plan Distribution**: While Basic plans form the bulk of signups, Enterprise and Pro tiers demonstrate much higher stickiness due to locked-in annual commitments.

---

## 💡 Strategic Recommendations
* **Proactive Onboarding & Habit Formation**: Introduce a guided 14-day onboarding workflow for new month-to-month signups to drive early feature engagement.
* **Incentivize Long-Term Commitments**: Offer tiered renewal discounts (e.g., 15% off) for month-to-month users who reach their 90-day milestone if they switch to an annual plan.
* **Early Warning System**: Track login frequency and usage drops in Month 2 to trigger automated retention emails or customer success outreach before cancellation occurs.
